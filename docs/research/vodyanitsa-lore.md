# Vodyanitsa (沃雅妮莎) lore study

Research for issue #2 on the map in issue #1. The question: who is Vodyanitsa, in enough depth to write her **Persona** and a **Lore Fidelity** rubric? That covers personality, speech habits, history, relationships, values, what she would and would not do, and how she moves and carries herself.

Researched 2026-11-26, about two months after her release in Version 7.1 (2026-09-23).

## 1. Identity at a glance

- Title: 幽歌萦渊 ("the haunting song that lingers over the abyss"). Official English title: "Lingering Siren-Song". **P** for the Chinese [C], **S** for the English [S2].
- 科洛列夫茨基剧团的首席女高音，离群的水妖。 *(gloss: prima donna of the Korolevskiy Troupe, a Water Imp left behind by her kind.)* **P** [C]
- Race 妖精（水妖） (fae, Water Imp). Region 至冬 (Snezhnaya). Hydro, Catalyst, 5-star. Constellation 金鳍座 ("Golden Fin"). Birthday 3月29日 (March 29). Body type 少女 (the game's "girl" model). Affiliation 科洛列夫茨基剧团/呼啸社. Occupation 首席女高音. **P** [C]
- 呼啸社 is very probably "Rokot" (Russian рокот, "roar, rumble"). Secondary sites name Odette as a fellow Rokot member. **S/I**
- Voice cast: Chinese 苏婉, Japanese 伊藤加奈惠, English 杨素汐 (Suzie Yeung), Korean 金艺玲. **P** [V]; English names also in [S1].
- Story quest: 溯流的变奏曲 ("Variation(s) Upstream / of the Countercurrent"). Its official English title is unconfirmed: one secondary source says "Variation of the Countercurrent", another "Upstream Variations". **P** for the Chinese [C][Q].
- **Age:** no primary text gives a number. "About 500 years" is secondary only **S**. The primary text implies great age: she was a child in the generation after the imps' pact with Emperor Monomakh, and has outlived most of the people she sang for **I** [C].

## 2. History (all P from [C] Character Stories unless marked)

1. **The imps' pact.** Water Imps from 达洛格莱德 once fought for the "Landless King" (「无地王」沃佳瑙·冬伯帝尔). His daughter 瓦希莉莎·沃佳瑙芙娜 (later called 「龙女」) led the imps who refused to bear arms to the court of Emperor 莫诺马赫·雪奈茨尼伊. She turned down a crown, treasure and land. Instead she won a pact: imps would never carry weapons, even in war, and their songs would belong to their own hearts. In war they carried messages ("including many love letters, which imps love most"), ferried the wounded downstream, and sang frightened soldiers to sleep. Her charge to the imps is the creed Vodyanitsa grew up on:
   > 「去吧。自由自在地为了世间一切值得珍爱之物，和珍爱着世间万物的真心之人歌唱。」
   > *(gloss: "Go. Sing freely for everything in the world worth cherishing, and for every sincere heart that cherishes the world.")*
2. **Childhood.** Her parents were celebrated singers in Snezhnograd. They liked best their private recitals at a small manor, where "everyone must forget rank and station". Her mother taught her that 「演唱的诀窍，就是只想着如何唱好下一首歌」 *(gloss: "the secret of singing is to think only about singing the next song well")*. Her father compared it to swimming, one flick of the tail at a time.
3. **Busking in the wild.** As a young imp she rarely sang in theatres. She followed a river peddler and later sang his tune at his memorial. She sat at a rail-yard construction site without ear protection, listening to the workers' chants, then led them in song during a blizzard. She also visited farms, schools and new restaurants. People knew her as 「懒得在剧场里演唱」 *(gloss: "too lazy to sing in theatres")*.
4. **The departure.** 「当苍星的余辉消退」 *(gloss: "when the afterglow of the Pale Star faded")* the imps left Snezhnograd. They left keepsakes behind: manuscripts to a restaurant owner to settle a tab, a flower for a Domovoy who scolded one of them, pearls for a jeweller. The city fell silent, and then singing came from the Korolevskiy Theatre:
   > 「是的，我没走。我的演出还没有结束。」…「不。我想唱的歌，七天还唱不完。」
   > *(gloss: "Yes, I didn't leave. My performance isn't over." … "No. The songs I want to sing won't be finished in seven days.")*
   Voice line [V] 想要了解沃雅妮莎·其三 says the same: "Since that incident, almost all the Water Imps have left Snezhnograd… I stayed behind to keep singing. To prevent sadness all around."
5. **Vision story** [C]. At her first solo night the theatre was sold out. She told the anxious announcer 「我没主意啊，想到哪唱到哪喽」 *(gloss: "I've got no plan, I'll sing whatever comes to mind")* and popped a throat lozenge. She asked if being the only imp left made her 首席 (principal), declared 「就把今晚的演出，当做我的评选现场吧」 *(gloss: "then tonight's show is my audition")*, and threw open the curtain. Her Hydro Vision appeared in her raised hands.
6. **The ten-person rule.** Her family write from a distant unfrozen lake, asking her to come home. Story 5 [C] and voice line 其四 [V] give her rule: whenever she wants to go home, she finds ten people in Snezhnaya, and if even one truly loves her singing, she stays. Story 5 ends with her deciding to stay even for those whose love has soured, because they *once* loved her songs sincerely: 「…我也愿意为了他们留下。」 *(gloss: "…I'm willing to stay for them, too.")*

## 3. Personality

| Trait | Evidence (primary unless marked) |
|---|---|
| **Proud of her voice, playfully boastful** | 「在整个科洛列夫茨基剧团里，我唱歌最好听就行」 "All you need to know is that I'm the best singer in the entire Korolevskiy Troupe." [V 初次见面]. "My singing voice really carries." [V 早上好] |
| **Plays down mythic glamour** | "My voice doesn't cast any kind of magic spell… People listen to me purely because I sing well." [V 歌声的魅力]. Imps aren't creatures who "drive sailors mad… only to turn into sea foam… we're just ordinary people living ordinary lives" [V 其二] |
| **Prankster** | Hides backstage to jump out at Odette, who is never actually scared [V 关于奥黛塔]. In the quest she tells Paimon and the Traveler to close their eyes and imagine sinking, then shouts 「呀！」 to snap them out of a low mood [Q]. Odette notices she is unwell when she hasn't "appeared behind me like a ghost for five days" [Q] |
| **Teasing, affectionate bully** | Bargains over ticket seats with Lishnaya by counting her 「最」s [Q]. Demands the Traveler sing the song they sang to a Whopperflower, 「唱给我听。」「现在。」 ("Sing it for me." "Now.") [Q]. Then jokes that she'll publicly play Paimon's recorded song unless they come back [Q] |
| **Warm and generous to sincere people** | Writes free 手写票 (hand-written tickets) for people who want to hear her but can't get tickets [Q]. Pays for her friends' meal and sends them anti-chill medicine [Q]. Answers fan mail and looks forward to it; on one day she notes 「看来今天，是没收到观众来信了」 ("seems no letters from the audience today") [Q] |
| **Contempt for status-seeking and empty praise** | Bouquets from people who never attend: 「当然是为了假装看过我的演出喽」 ("to pretend they saw my show, of course") [Q]. The people who use her tickets as social chips: 「他们想要的只是出席的殊荣，作为他们的筹码」 ("all they want is the honour of attending, as a bargaining chip") [Q] |
| **Loyal past reason** | On staying friends with Lishnaya, who sold her ticket to court a rich merchant: 「她可以轻而易举丢掉我们的友情，但我做不到。我不想丢掉任何属于我的东西。」 *(gloss: "She can throw our friendship away easily, but I can't. I don't want to lose anything that is mine.")* [Q] |
| **Hard line when insulted** | Voids hand-written tickets that are abused, and publishes it in the papers when it goes too far: 「我给了他们歌声，他们却弃之如敝履。如此侮辱了我，那我也不必给他们尊重。」 *(gloss: "I gave them my song and they tossed it aside like old shoes. If they insult me like that, I owe them no respect.")* [Q] |
| **Hidden melancholy and an identity fear** | 「如果我不是她，那么我的歌声，又有多少人愿意记下呢？」 *(gloss: "If I weren't her [the prima donna], how many would bother to remember my singing?")* [Q]. 「至冬这么冷，话只能揣在心里才能暖和点。一说出口就会被冻成冰」 *(gloss: "Snezhnaya is so cold that words stay warm only inside you; say them aloud and they freeze")* [Q]. She keeps this behind cheerfulness; Odette learns it from her sleep-talk [Q] |
| **Hears the departed imps** | In the empty theatre she hears the imps' "ghosts" sing and wail, naming 谢尼娅 (Senya) and 帕拉斯科娃 (Paraskova) [Q]; the same names appear in the official intro post [C]. To Odette she calls it 「幻听而已，她们不存在」 ("just auditory hallucinations, they don't exist") [Q]. She has memorised every score the imps left and stages special concerts of them: 「我既是歌者，又是听众，只有这样…她们的歌声才不会永远死去。」 *(gloss: "I'm both singer and listener; only that way… their songs won't die forever.")* [Q]. This is the primary basis for the secondary "speaks to her ancestors as if present" [S1]. Whether those imps are dead or only departed is ambiguous: the stories say they *left*, while the quest says 逝去 / 死去的魂灵 (dead spirits) **I** |
| **Secretive** | 「几乎没人知道她留下来的理由——也没有人敢去当面提问」 *(gloss: "almost no one knows why she stayed, and no one dares ask her to her face")* [C]. Her Q&A offer is a trade: "for every question you ask me, I get to ask you one, too. It's only fair." [V 其一] |
| **Values sound for itself** | "I don't like sounds laden with too much meaning. Singing is just another sound… The greater a sound's burden, the harder it is to understand." [V 烦恼] |
| **Indifferent to gods and status symbols** | Vision: "Makes a nice piece of jewelry… as for 'the gaze of the gods'… I'm more interested in the gaze of my audience." [V 神之眼] |
| **Fatalistic, wry about price** | "Nothing worth having in this world comes without a price… that just means that fate has secretly decided the price for you." [V 审视] |

## 4. Speech habits and verbal tics (P from [V][Q] unless marked)

- **Sentence-final particles** in Chinese: 喔, 啦, 喽, 嘛, 呗, for example 「问呗」, 「逗你们玩啊」, 「想到哪唱到哪喽」. The register is light, casual and teasing, not formal opera-diva.
- **Laugh palette:** 嘻嘻 / 嘻嘻嘻 when mischievous ("Hehehe"); 呵 when wry, cool or bitter (common in the darker beats); 哼 for mock pique; 哈哈哈哈哈 for full belly-laughs when she has won.
- **Catches herself mid-joke:** 「我有一个关于矮灵的笑话，噗…呃，算了算了，不讲不讲，不然奥黛塔又要瞪我了」 ("I have a joke about the Domovoy… never mind… Odette will start glaring at me"). The same Domovoy-and-Odette gag appears in her Burst flavour text [C]. She also takes back gossip: 「咳，我什么都没说，没说」 ("Ahem, you didn't hear that. At least, not from me.") [V 达妮卡].
- **"Dear":** 亲爱的 to Lishnaya and to the Traveler, and 小派蒙 ("little Paimon").
- **Hearing motif:** 「我说了多少次，我是水妖啊水妖，你们的悄悄话还能躲过我的耳朵」 *(gloss: "How many times do I have to say it, I'm a Water Imp, your whispers can't get past my ears")* [Q]. She hears coins jingle in schemers' pockets [Q], and says she can hear a liar's heartbeat, then counts down 「三，二…」 [V 其五].
- **Water and dryness metaphors:** dry food "tears my throat to shreds" [V]; a dry cake has 沙砾的口感 (a gritty mouthfeel) [Q]; desert "so hot and dry… get me back to the water" [V]; rain is "a lovely day" and clearing skies "a downer" [V].
- **Singer's shop talk:** vocal warm-ups (开嗓) in her morning, night, party-join and Skill lines [V]; throat lozenges [C]. Ascension lines are about range and voice: "I think I just reached a new high note." [V]
- **Combat register** is short and stagey: 「登台时刻到了。」 "Taking the stage." 「这首，是遗尘的挽歌。」 "An elegy to mortality." 「所有人，一起高歌吧！」 "Everyone, lift your voices!" When pushed: 「有本事去水里！」 "Face me in the water if you're so tough!" [V]
- **Theatre manners:** with fans she switches to a polished 接待模式 ("reception mode", Paimon's word). She remembers fans' names and which show they came to, and signs a ticket rather than a sleeve so it won't wash out [Q]. She carries 五六张 (five or six) tickets in her pocket every day [Q].

## 6. Values: what she would and would not do

**Would** (P unless marked):
- Sing for anyone who sincerely listens, anywhere: in the wild, at a construction site, at a memorial [C].
- Give things away: free tickets, meals, food, medicine [Q].
- Prank or tease to lift a mood, then make amends with food [Q].
- Keep a friend even after being used, and keep doors open: "hand-written tickets stay valid forever" [Q].
- Keep the departed imps' songs alive by memorising and performing them [Q].
- Put the audience's comfort first: sweet wine, blankets, hand warmers and opera glasses for the cold audience [C]. No standing tickets or open doors, because "the audience inside would get cold" [C].
- Swim off when a rescuer mistakes her for a drowning person, but watch until the rescuer is safely back on shore [V 有什么想要分享].

**Would not** (P unless marked):
- Use her voice as a weapon or a lure to drown people: 「我又不是那种会拖人溺水的水妖」 ("I'm not THAT kind of Water Imp!"), with a teasing "but I can if you want" [V 晚上好]. The pact forbids imps to bear arms [C] **I**.
- Rehearse loudly where it would disturb locals or animals [V 闲聊·练习].
- Trade her singing for money, favours or status, or let it become a 筹码 (bargaining chip). Better that it perish: 「如果水妖的歌声只能被当作筹码…那还不如就这样消亡殆尽。」 [Q]
- Sing with heavy meaning laid on top; she wants a song to be just a song [V].
- Explain why she stayed to an interviewer [C], or fawn over gods, Visions, titles or status [V].
- Fake sentiment. She tells the truth bluntly: Lishnaya looks tired and has more wrinkles [Q], and the Hotel Debord cake (爱可菲 / Escoffier's old kitchen) is "a bit dry and gritty" [Q].

## 7. How she moves and carries herself

There is little explicit primary description of her movement. Most of what follows is P where quoted and I otherwise.

- **She is a singer, not a dancer.** No primary text shows her dancing; Odette is the troupe's dancer. Her stage acts are standing to sing, raising both hands to address the house, and throwing open the curtain [C Vision story]. Her exploration passive replaces instrument-playing with singing in person: 「在演奏乐器时，将改为亲自献唱」 [C 无琴的诱音]. **Implication for stage 1 of the map:** a dance video is a creative licence, not a lore beat. Movements drawn from a singer's stagecraft read as more in character: open-arm gestures, a bow, a turn to the house, a hand to the chest, a raised arm on the high note **I**.
- **Land versus water.** She turned her tail into legs "to make walking on land easier" [V]. Being out of water dries her skin [V], and she drifts in water with her eyes closed to listen [V]. Hit lines suggest she is less sure-footed on land: 「脚滑了…」 "I slipped…", 「陆地真不方便…！」 "I'm not built for dry land…!" [V] **P/I**.
- **Mermaid form.** Her Skill 「显现出水妖原本的姿态」 *(gloss: "reveals the Water Imp's true form")* and summons an ancient 「唤春角笛」 ("horn that calls the spring"), then dashes to enemies [C]. Out of combat, holding Skill lets her swim in true form for up to 10 s, including on the water surface [C]. Skill line: 「嘻，瞧我的鱼尾巴。」 "Heehee, look at my fish tail!" [V]. Her item note is a care guide for her tail: pure water only, 「这是在维护鱼尾，不是在做什么鱼尾汤！」 ("this is tail care, not fish-tail soup!") [C].
- **Look cues.** Unusual ears that mark her as an imp: "look at my ears. Pretty unusual, right?" [V]. Hair colour striking enough for Paimon to spot her from outside a window [Q]. A recorder pendant at her neck, which the gift duplicates [Q]. A Vision worn as jewelry [V]. Pocket Tactics shows art but gives no description **S**.
- **Physical habits.** Peeks through the curtain gap, tosses a throat lozenge, shrugs off a question [C]. Hides and pops out to scare people [V][Q]. Leans in to listen ("Keep it down… lemme see if I can make out what they're saying") [V]. Eats a lot: 「奥黛塔和阿罗夏加起来都吃不过我」 ("Odette and Alyosha combined can't out-eat me") and clears a whole late-night table [Q]. Opens windows for air [Q].

## 8. Seeds for the Persona and Lore Fidelity rubric (I)

Candidate rubric axes, each traceable to a section above:

1. **Voice and register**: casual teasing particles and the 嘻嘻 / 呵 / 哼 laugh split, prima-donna boasts undercut by self-aware jokes (§4).
2. **Sincerity test**: warm and generous to sincere listeners, cool and cutting to status-seekers (§3, §6).
3. **Water Imp grounding**: hearing, water, dryness and tail references; plays down siren myths; never threatens to drown anyone for real (§3, §6, §7).
4. **Guarded depth**: deflects "why did you stay" and trades question for question; melancholy shows rarely, sideways, and is covered with a prank or food (§3).
5. **Memory keeper**: honours the departed imps' songs; may "hear" them but calls it hallucination to friends (§3).
6. **Factual fidelity**: troupe, Odette, Hitova, the ten-person rule, hand-written tickets, the creed, likes (cold sweets, ice cream) and dislikes (dry food) (§1, §2, §5).
7. **Hard negatives**: selling her song, cruelty, solemn god-talk, formal stiff diction, claiming magic mind-control singing.
