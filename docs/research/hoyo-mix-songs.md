# HOYO-MiX songs as the source of her Singing Performance

Research for issue #21 ("Her singing: what she performs and with whose voice"). It builds on `docs/research/songs-and-their-terms.md` (branch `research/songs-and-their-terms`) and does not repeat it. The question this time: which songs by miHoYo's music team HOYO-MiX could Vodyanitsa (沃雅妮莎) sing in a Singing Performance, either to the official recording or in a synthetic voice of her own from a score, and on what terms.

Researched 2026-11-27. This is fact-finding, not legal advice.

**Labels.**
- **P**: read on a primary source. That means HOYO-MiX's own release metadata as served by Apple's catalogue (iTunes Search/Lookup API) and NetEase Cloud Music (album and lyric endpoints, whose lyric files carry HOYO-MiX's own credit block), a rights holder's own page, or the artist's own channel.
- **S**: rests on secondary pages only (news, fan reposts, search snippets).
- **I**: my own inference from what was read.

None of the audio was listened to and none was downloaded, so every "style" entry below is **I**. It is inferred from credits, language, title and lyric text.

### 1b. "A Winter Night's Aria" / 「寒夜咏叹调」 (also 冬夜咏叹调). No audio release found

- **What it is.** The 7.1 instalment of the 「雪落有声 · 至冬主题文化共创系列活动」 ("Melody of the Snowfall") culture series, episode II, billed as an "opera collaboration" (歌剧合作). **S**
- **Singer.** Daria Terekhova, leading soprano of the Stanislavsky Theatre, Moscow. She sings as the lead diva of Snezhnaya's royal opera, that is, as Vodyanitsa. **S**
- **Official video.** The singer's own YouTube channel carries "Daria Terekhova. Vodyanitsa 'A Winter Night's Aria' from Genshin Impact" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mBrdeDj4Vwo). **P** that the upload exists, read via YouTube oEmbed. Its description, length and credits could not be read.
- **Repost.** A Bilibili repost by a non-official account (BV1AKap6nEUs) runs 1:45. Its description says "7.1版本由斯坦尼剧院首席女高音Daria Terekhova为旅行者带来优美的演唱". **P** for the repost's own metadata, **S** as to the official original.
- **Missing details.** No lyricist, composer, language or album was found on any page read. It is very probably Russian and very probably a bel-canto treatment of the "I Loved You" material **I**: the earlier study's ixbt interview has Terekhova speak of the Pushkin romance.
- **No audio release.** It is absent from HOYO-MiX's Apple catalogue (166 albums listed by artist ID 1447413190, newest 2026-11-17) and from HOYO-MiX's NetEase album list (168 albums, newest 2026-11-18). An Apple song search for "Daria Terekhova" returns only her 2012 film-score work. So **no official audio release exists as of 2026-11-27**. **P** (absence in both catalogues)
- **No instrumental**, and no published lyrics, were found.

## 4. Scores

**Official.**
- No HOYO-MiX or miHoYo publication of sheet music with a vocal line and lyrics was found for any shortlisted song. **S** (absence)
- miHoYo's **Impact4Music** programme gives "selected original sheet music" free to **non-profit orchestras that apply** (press release, 2024-09-12, gamespress.com). It is orchestral, by application, and not public. **S**
- MuseScore.com lists an **"Official"** (licensed) score "Vodyanitsa's Song [Genshin Impact] - HOYO-MiX (Piano)" by Made By Muse. It is solo piano, 1:19, very probably "I Loved You". **P** that the listing exists, read in the browser. Its terms are MuseScore's licensed-score terms (not read). It has **no vocal line**.

**Fan transcriptions with a vocal line.**
- **La vaguelette:** MuseScore community score 「轻涟 La vaguelette」 by a community uploader, **Piano-Voice**, 2:12. **P** for the listing. MuseScore files are .mscz, exportable to MusicXML, MIDI and PDF. The licence was not read, and community uploads of copyrighted songs carry no licence from HOYO-MiX **I**.
  - A Bilibili column gives 简谱 for 风物之诗琴 only (cv27651860). **S**
- **I Loved You:** apart from the official piano listing, no vocal transcription was found in a quick search. MuseScore's search shows a "Voice" instrument filter among about nine "vodyanitsa" results, but which result it marks was not opened.
- **Everything else on the shortlist:** not searched one by one.
- **The practical route** for a score to drive her synthetic voice is a hand or AI transcription from the official recording into MusicXML. The result is a derivative of a copyrighted work, so it falls under section 5 **I**.

## 5. The rules for using HOYO-MiX music

### Global (HoYoverse, outside mainland China). P (from the earlier study, not re-read: the help page returned HTTP 403 today)

- **Help Center** ("Can I use in-game music for my fan-made content?"): "HoYoverse's in-game music is limited to personal, non-commercial use."
- **Legal FAQ** (Genshin Impact Official, HoYoLAB, https://www.hoyolab.com/article/143107):
  - Use of "character voice-overs/character music, game music or voice samples" "is limited to personal non-commercial use".
  - The declaration "© All rights reserved by COGNOSPHERE. Other properties belong to their respective owners." is required.
  - Where voice-over personnel are involved you must get their confirmation yourself.
  - You may not sell or distribute any musical work containing the game's music.
  - Removal can be requested "at any time".
  - Video sites: non-commercial, except platform partner programmes. You must add your own creative work, and plain soundtrack reposts are not allowed.

### Mainland China (米哈游). No music or 翻唱 rule found from a primary source

- The only Genshin mainland guideline found is about physical goods: 《原神同人周边正式运行指引》 V1.3, read via a fan Weibo repost dated 2025-04-11. **S**
  - Its preamble lets players "自由对《原神》系列已公开发表的内容进行二次独创".
  - Region: "中国大陆，港澳台地区除外".
  - It has **no clause on music, audio, voice acting, video or covers**.
  - It refuses filings for AI-made works: "暂不接受任何由AI制作（含生成式AI）的同人作品稿件素材相关申报".
  - Official copies exist on act.mihoyo.com (同人周边申报官网), ys.mihoyo.com (V1.0) and 米游社 (V1.1, V1.2), seen as Bing results only. 米游社 search did not render in the browser.
- A 游戏合规 article on Sohu (2026-06-09) quotes, without naming the guideline: "使用官方角色配音、背景音乐等音频素材，仅可用于非商业用途，若涉及配音人员相关权利，需自行获得配音人员授权". This matches the 崩坏：星穹铁道 guideline's audio clause reported in the earlier study. **S**
- **No HOYO-MiX notice authorising covers (翻唱) or 二次创作 of its songs was found** on any page read, in Chinese or English searches. **S** (absence)

### Applied to her Singing Performance

- **(a) Official recording as her soundtrack.**
  - Shown privately: within "personal, non-commercial use" **P/I**.
  - Posted to YouTube unmonetised with the © COGNOSPHERE line and her own animation: allowed by the global FAQ, but removable at any time **P/I**.
  - Posted to Bilibili: governed by mainland practice. The only wording found (S) is the same non-commercial audio clause **S/I**.
  - Either way, "I Loved You" and "Dear Odette" use the singer's actual voice, which the FAQ says needs the voice performer's own confirmation **P/I**.
- **(b) Cover: re-sung over an official or self-made accompaniment.**
  - No rule found speaks to covers. A cover is a new musical work containing the game's music, and the global FAQ forbids selling or distributing those **P**.
  - Private viewing is fine **I**.
  - A posted, unmonetised cover is tolerated in practice but not licensed. It needs the © line and can be taken down **I**.
- **(c) AI or synthetic voice singing the song.**
  - No HoYoverse or HOYO-MiX music rule mentions AI singing **S** (absence).
  - Related signals:
    - The mainland goods guideline refuses AI-made works **S**.
    - The Shanghai Pudong court held that commercially selling AI voice packs imitating Genshin characters' timbre was unfair competition (¥750,000, reported 2026-09-10, final in November) **S**.
  - A synthetic voice of her own, **not cloned** from 说说Crystal, Terekhova or her voice actresses, singing a HOYO-MiX song is legally a cover in a new voice, so (b) applies. A voice cloned from the official singers is the risky case **I**.

### Platform practice. S
- No report was found of HOYO-MiX itself filing YouTube Content ID claims against fan videos or covers.
- Two incidents of third-party claims on Genshin material were reported:
  - A fraudulent claimant blocked Genshin soundtrack videos, including the composer's own (cybersport.metaratings.ru, 2025-06-25).
  - FUTURE TV Co., Ltd. claims blocked Japanese Genshin streams, with the work claimed unknown (gaming-st.com, 2026-09-06).
- Nothing reliable on Bilibili removals was found.
