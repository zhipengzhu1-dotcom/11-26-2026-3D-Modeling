# Songs she could sing, and their terms

Research for issue #21 ("Her singing: what she performs and with whose voice"). The question: which songs could Vodyanitsa (沃雅妮莎) perform, what are the usage terms for each, and what recordings, scores and singing motions exist to build a performance from.

Researched 2026-11-27. This is fact-finding, not legal advice.

**Labels.** **P** means read on the primary source (the rights holder's own page, the file's own licence page, the work's own catalogue page). **S** means it rests on secondary pages only (news, fan uploads, search snippets). **I** means my own inference from what was read.

- **Style (S/I).** Operatic bel canto. In Terekhova's account (ixbt), the romance section echoes Pushkin's "I loved you", and the fast second half reminded her of the coloratura section of Glière's *Concerto for Coloratura Soprano*. Early on it also reminded her of the Swan Princess in *The Tale of Tsar Saltan*. In short, this is a lyric-coloratura soprano writing in a Russian romance idiom. That matches her lore role of 首席女高音 (lead soprano).
- **「山与雀」 ("The Mountain Sparrow").** Searches in Chinese and English found no audio for it, and no audio for the Water Imps' old songs (Senya's, Paraskova's). In the lore they exist only as titles and as story. **S** (absence of evidence only)
- **Usage of all of the above:** everything in this table is HoYoverse music, so section 2 governs it. Her official voice also belongs to a named voice actor (Chinese 苏婉, Japanese 伊藤加奈惠, English 杨素汐, per `docs/research/vodyanitsa-lore.md`) and, for the aria, to an uncredited singer.

## 2. HoYoverse's rules for fan works using game music or vocals

**Global (outside mainland China): read in full. P**
- HoYoverse Help Center, "Can I use in-game music for my fan-made content?" (https://support.hoyoverse.com/hc/en-us/articles/50333834244633), updated about a year ago: "HoYoverse's in-game music is limited to personal, non-commercial use." It links to the Legal FAQ.
- "Legal FAQ", Genshin Impact Official on HoYoLAB, 2021-01-20 (https://www.hoyolab.com/article/143107). It covers Genshin Impact as published by COGNOSPHERE, excluding mainland China. Point 2:
  - Non-commercial personal re-creation is "not prohibit[ed]", but no rights are transferred and it is not "an approval in the legal sense (including implied approval)". Works must carry the declaration "© All rights reserved by COGNOSPHERE. Other properties belong to their respective owners."
  - For "character voice-overs/character music, game music or voice samples", use "is limited to personal non-commercial use" and the declaration must be shown. If voice-over personnel are involved, you must get their confirmation yourself. You may not sell or distribute any musical work containing the game's music. HoYoverse "reserve[s] the right to request you to remove the content related to Genshin Impact music materials at any time".
  - Unreleased (leaked) material is excluded entirely.
- Point 3 (in-game footage on video sites): footage is non-commercial only, except platform partner programmes (YouTube, Twitch and similar). You may not imply official endorsement. Third-party music needs its own authorisation. You must add your own creative ideas, and plain reposts of soundtracks or trailers are not allowed.

**What that means for this project (I).**
- **Shown privately**, a fan video that uses her official aria is personal non-commercial use, so it is allowed with the © COGNOSPHERE declaration.
- **Posted to YouTube** (global terms), it is still allowed if it is unmonetised (the partner programme is the only permitted earning), carries the declaration, adds original work, and claims no affiliation. It can be removed on request at any time. "If your use involves the authorization of any voice-over personnel, please acquire confirmation from the said personnel involved yourself" makes reusing the actual sung or spoken voice the weakest point.
- **Posted to Bilibili**, the mainland rules apply, and their exact text was not found (see below).
- **Making her sing in a voice cloned from the official voice** is a separate and much riskier matter. In September 2026 the Shanghai Pudong court ordered an AI voice-changer app to stop offering paid voice packs for 63 Genshin characters, and to pay miHoYo ¥750,000. The court treated a character's timbre as a 商业标识 (commercial identifier) under unfair-competition law (IT之家, https://www.ithome.com/1/000/573.htm, 2026-09-10). **S** That case was commercial, but it bears directly on the "with whose voice" half of #21.

**Mainland China (国服): not found.** No 米哈游 page with the equivalent music clause could be read. Pages found but not used: a third-party page (zh.accio.com) paraphrasing a clause identical to the global one, a 游戏合规 article on Sohu quoting the 崩坏：星穹铁道 fan-work guideline ("官方配音、背景音乐等音频仅可用于非商业用途"), and the 原神同人周边 merchandise guidelines (V1.2/V1.3), which cover physical goods. Trying to open 米游社 search in the browser did not load. **S only.**

**Machine-readable scores with the vocal line.**
- **Ave Maria: yes, clean. P.**
  - OpenScore Lieder Corpus, `scores/Schubert,_Franz/Op.52/6_Ellens_Gesang_III,_D.839_(Ave_Maria)/lc6389103.mxl` (MusicXML, plus .mscx). The repository licence is CC0-1.0 (https://github.com/OpenScore/Lieder, checked through the GitHub API).
  - Also Mutopia piece 1054, voice and piano, Public Domain, with LilyPond and MIDI (https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=1054).
- **Vocalise: PDFs only. P.** IMSLP has PD scans (Muzyka about 1967, Hawkes 1916 voice and orchestra) and typeset CC BY 3.0 PDFs in several keys (#114815, #72743, and others). No MIDI or MusicXML of the voice-and-piano original is listed.
- **Song to the Moon: scans only. P.** IMSLP has PD vocal scores (Umělecké besedy 1910) and a PD full score and parts (Kalmus reprint). "Synthesized/MIDI: 0". One vocal selection is flagged "Non-PD US, Non-PD EU" (Muzgiz 1983).
- **The Nightingale: scans only. P.** IMSLP has PD scores (Schuberth about 1857, Ditson about 1866, Jurgenson 1901) and Glinka's voice-and-orchestra version (PD). The only engraving files are Jürgen Knuth's mixed-chorus arrangement (Capella, CC BY 3.0), which is not the solo line.
- **Volkhova, Snegurochka, Dargomyzhsky:** IMSLP has opera pages (*Sadko*, *The Snow Maiden*), but the file lists were not read.
- For pieces with scans only, the route is optical music recognition of a PD scan, or a hand transcription, which would itself be a new CC0 file **I**. MuseScore.com community uploads were not checked one by one; their licences vary per upload.
