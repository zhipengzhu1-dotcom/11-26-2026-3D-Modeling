# Her Voice is designed, and her official songs keep their recordings

She needs a voice to speak with in a Live Session and a way to sing as the lead soprano of her lore. Her Voice is synthetic and her own: designed from a written description, steered by the human's Ear Check toward her Chinese in-game voice, with no real person's recordings fed in. Her own official songs are given as Singing Performances to the official recordings exactly as released, and her Voice sings only what the game never recorded her singing. We chose this over cloning her voice actor, which would be closest to the game, because a voice made from someone's recordings needs that person's consent, HoYoverse's rules leave that consent to the fan, and a court has already treated cloned Genshin voices as the game's own identifiers. We chose the official recordings over re-singing her songs in her Voice because they are her real singing voice at a level no synthetic route reaches, no singing tool with an operatic voice sings Russian, and re-voicing them would alter a singer's recorded performance.

## Consequences

- She has two voices, as she does in the game: a singer's in her official songs and her designed Voice in speech. Her own Voice therefore sings lightly, in Mandarin or wordless, in a comfortable range; it is judged as the same person who speaks, singing pleasantly, never as a trained soprano.
- "Closer to her Chinese in-game voice" is a direction for the description, not a target to be matched. A recognisable likeness is a risk only if her Voice is posted, so it is weighed again before any post that carries it.
- The only recordings ever used to make her speak are her own designed clips. Those clips fix her Voice and are Kept Results; losing them loses the way she sounds.
- The official tracks are bought by the human and stay out of this public repo with the other downloaded originals. A vocal separated from a track by code is used only to work out her mouth shapes; what is heard is the track as released.
- A posted Singing Performance stands on HoYoverse's footing for fan videos: unmonetised, its copyright notice shown, removable at any time. Public-domain pieces are the fallback for anything that must be safe to post.
- Every song in her Songbook is made by code, so singing tools that can only be worked by hand in an editor are ruled out, however good their voices.
