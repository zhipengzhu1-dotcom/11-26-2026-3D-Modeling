# Her bounce is put into what she trains against

Training rewards closeness to a target, so a Controller that learns to anticipate the motion irons her Q弹 out: in the first look her overshoot fell from 3 to 4% (11% in the walk) to under 1% once the motion's own joint forces were fed forward. We therefore put the bounce into the target. She trains against a Sprung Motion, which is the Fitted Motion as her own soft arms, head and chest perform it with her hips carried, the playback the human already tunes by Eye Check. We chose this over leaving the bounce to her body under loose scoring, which guarantees nothing, and over scoring bounce directly, which is hard to define and easy for training to cheat.

## Consequences

- Her legs follow the plain Fitted Motion, so her feet land where the choreography puts them. How she lands and rebounds comes from real physics and is judged at the Eye Check.
- A change to her springiness changes the Sprung Motion. It shows at once with her hips carried, and under real physics only after a top-up Training Run.
- How far she strays is measured from the Sprung Motion, so the bounce the human approved is never counted as an error. The Eye Check still shows her beside the Fitted Motion.
- A Build makes the Sprung Motion from the Fitted Motion and her springiness numbers. A kept Controller is tied to the Sprung Motion it was trained on.
- Training her against the plain Fitted Motion to bring her error down would undo this decision.
