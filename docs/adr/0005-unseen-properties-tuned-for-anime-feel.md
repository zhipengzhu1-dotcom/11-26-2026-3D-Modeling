# Her unseen properties are tuned for Anime Feel, not for likeness to a real person

Her Simulation Body could have been made like a real woman's (real weight fractions, real joint ranges, a dancer's strength), or adjusted until balancing was easy. We chose neither, because she is an anime-game character and should move like one. What is seen never changes: her joints, limb lengths, heels and outline are exactly the model's. What is unseen (weight, strength, springiness, joint range, number of joints) is chosen so she reads as alive, flexible, Q弹 and 可爱, judged by the human's Eye Check. When goals pull apart, staying upright comes first, Anime Feel second, and matching the Fitted Motion exactly third.

## Consequences

- Her weight comes from her own drawn body at uniform density, recomputed by the Build: about 25.5 kg, with her head a sixth of it where a real woman's is a fifteenth. This is deliberate and is not to be "corrected" to human fractions.
- Her joints bend past a real person's range (knees and elbows never backward) and are soft and springy, not stiff and exact. Her strength is set so she looks light on her feet, not by what a person her size could do.
- She has 19 parts where the usual template has 15: a chest, a neck, two collarbones and moving wrists are added, because her character lives in her upper body.
- Her shoe touches the floor only where it is drawn, on a front pad and a stiletto tip, and each foot is one rigid piece. A filled wedge is the one fallback and needs the human's go-ahead.
- Her structure (parts, joints, collision solids, shoe contact) is locked by an Eye Check before any long training, because a trained controller is tied to it. Her numbers (weight, strength, springiness) stay open to feedback.
